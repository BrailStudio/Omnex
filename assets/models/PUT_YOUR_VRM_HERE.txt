Put your avatar file here as: omnex.vrm

Where to get a free VRM anime avatar:
1. VRoid Studio (https://vroid.com/en/studio) — free desktop app, build/customize
   a character and export as .vrm directly.
2. VRoid Hub (https://hub.vroid.com) — browse and download VRM models made by
   others (check each model's usage license).
3. Ready Player Me (https://readyplayer.me) — generates a .glb avatar; convert
   to .vrm with the VRM Add-on for Blender if you want the VRM-specific
   expression blendshapes (used for lip sync in this app).

Rename the exported file to omnex.vrm and drop it in this folder, then
restart the app.
